Selecting A Goldfish

When trying to grow a monstrous goldfish, it is highly encouraged to pick a goldfish from a reputable place such as a specialized goldfish store or an aquatic pet store. There will be more variety, quality and higher possibility for that goldfish to be a monster size. Majority of the goldfish from those kind of places usually carry show grade or offsprings of show grade parent goldfish. Meaning that the likelihood of their babies being giants are very high. It is a investment to make but it worth it for a greater chance of growing a huge goldfish. Although it is still possible to potentially grow a monstrous sized goldfish from a big chained pet store but it is more unpredictable.

Feeding Goldfish

The food diet of the goldfish plays a big role as well. They are like body builders, where they need a lot of protein to grow big. Therefore fish flakes will not support them to be grow to the maximum and can stunt their growth. To further support their growth, they should be on a high protein balanced goldfish food. Other types of foods to enrich their diets adding on top of their balance food is frozen protein, super green gel powder mixes, and vegetables. It is important to find a balanced goldfish food as well as having it be extra in protein or adding in extra protein so that they are getting all the necessary nutrients they need.

Water Quality

With growing a gigantic goldfish there will be gigantic poop. It is bound that there will be more poop since they are being fed a special diet as well as just more in amount as well. If they're water quality is not regularly kept fresh and clean it can stress which can stunt their growth. To prevent this, having a routine set schedule for cleaning their aquarium is advised. Another option could also doubling their aquarium space. Meaning, do not over stock the aquarium. With it being under stock there will be less poop, less poop means better water quality.
